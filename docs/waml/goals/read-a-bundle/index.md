# Read a Bundle

A reader opens a bundle and finds their way around it.

* [Open a Bundle](./open-a-bundle.md) - A reader gets a bundle on screen from a folder, a recent entry, or a link.
* [Browse the Tree](./browse-the-tree.md) - A reader sees the bundle's structure and opens documents from it.
* [Read a Document](./read-a-document.md) - A reader reads an OKF document as formatted prose, not as source.
* [Read a Diagram](./read-a-diagram.md) - A reader reads a UML document as a drawn diagram.
* [Navigate and Return](./navigate-and-return.md) - A reader follows a link and comes back.
* [Fit the Window](./fit-the-window.md) - The editor stays usable from a phone-width viewport to a wide desktop one.
